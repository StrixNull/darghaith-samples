# فكّر للحرمين 2026 — مشاركة الفريق

Working folder for our entry in **فكّر للحرمين 2026** (ديوان الابتكار — مؤسسة مسك × الهيئة العامة
للعناية بشؤون المسجد الحرام والمسجد النبوي), sixth edition, launched 23 Aug 2026.

**Our entry:** هَوْنًا إلى الروضة — *Hawnan: Gently to the Rawdah* — a silent, Authority-owned field
layer for the last 60 minutes before a Rawdah visit: a mother-tongue journey message hours before
the permit, one verification then a signed offline batch pass, an honest holding card with the
batch number and remaining-time range, a one-tap silent multilingual instruction channel for field
staff, and a digital twin driven by the same engine.

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `docs/00-idea-form.md` | Paste-ready Arabic idea form (idea, objectives, expected impact, team) for the Diwan registration |
| `docs/01-challenges-knowledge-base.md` | Our consolidated reading of the five official challenge briefs (12 challenges, 5 tracks), the team profile, and program logistics |
| `docs/02-track-analysis.md` | Per-challenge analysis of all 12 challenges, composite ranking, the 9 concepts that were judged, and why A1-1 won |
| `docs/03-concept.md` | The chosen concept: idea-form text, problem in numbers, solution, engineering plan, Diwan-phase scope, demo script, field research plan, KPIs, risks, pitch outline, team plan |
| `prototype/` | Working prototype: pure-Python engine (`hawnan_core`), FastAPI service, guest / staff / display / ops / check-in pages, content packs in 10 languages, tests |
| `pitch/` | Jury deck (`.pptx`, regenerable from `build-deck.js`) and the Arabic talk track with jury Q&A |

The official challenge PDFs are **not** committed here (organizer material). Keep them in the team drive.

Rules we work by (carried over from the Amad 2026 entry): no overclaiming what is built, every
simulated data source is declared as simulated, the demo never depends on the network, Western
digits everywhere, and no AI-generated religious text at runtime.
